FAQ

The questions we get on every first call.

Why unbranded questions?

A branded question already contains the answer. Ask an engine "is Brightwave good for hospitals" and it will find something kind to say. Your buyer types "best EHR for a 200-bed hospital" — and the engine picks two or three names. Unbranded is the only phrasing where being named means something.

How is this different from rank tracking?

A rank tracker tells you where a page sits in a list of blue links. There is no list here — there is one paragraph naming two vendors. We measure whether that paragraph says your name, and we keep the paragraph.

Does the score actually move?

Yes. Readings run on a cadence and are stored per question, per engine, per day, so you can see the effect of a specific page going live. The tracked set only ever grows additively, so history stays comparable.

Where do the rivals come from?

The answers themselves. Every brand co-mentioned or cited alongside you is collected and ranked by how often it takes a recommendation you wanted. You never maintain a competitor list — and the surprises are the point.

What happens to my data?

Scans are stored against your domain so trend history works. The questions are generated from public information about your category — we never need access to your analytics, your site, or your CRM.
