04

Reasonable objections

The ones that come up in every first call.

Why unbranded questions? Wouldn't branded ones tell me more?

A branded question already contains the answer. Ask an engine "is Brightwave good for hospitals" and it will find something kind to say. Your buyer does not type that — they type "best EHR for a 200-bed hospital", and the engine picks two or three names. Unbranded is the only phrasing where being named means something.

How is this different from rank tracking?

A rank tracker tells you where a page sits in a list of links. There is no list of links here — there is one paragraph naming two vendors. The unit of measurement is whether that paragraph says your name, so we measure that directly and keep the paragraph.

Does the score move, or is it a vanity number?

It moves. The reading runs on a cadence and is stored per question, per engine, per day, so you can see the effect of a specific page going live. When a question flips from a rival to you, we can point at the source the engine started citing.

Where do the rivals come from?

The answers themselves. Every brand co-mentioned or cited alongside you is collected and ranked by how often it takes a recommendation you wanted. You do not maintain a competitor list, and the surprises are the point.
