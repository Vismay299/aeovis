Objections

Answered without marketing language.

Why unbranded questions?

A branded question already contains the answer. Ask an engine “is Brightwave good for hospitals” and it will find something kind to say — it is a completion machine and you handed it the premise. Your buyer types “best EHR for a 200-bed hospital”, and there the engine actually has to choose. Unbranded is the only phrasing where being named means anything.

How is this different from rank tracking?

A rank tracker tells you where a page sits in a list of blue links. There is no list here — there is one paragraph naming two vendors. The unit of measurement is whether that paragraph says your name, so we measure that directly and keep the paragraph.

Does the score actually move?

Yes, and that is why it runs on a cadence. Readings are stored per question, per engine, per day. The tracked set only ever grows additively, so a reading from March is still comparable to one from August even after the set has expanded.

What do you need access to?

A domain. Nothing else. The questions are generated from public information about your category — we never need your analytics, your CMS, or your CRM.
