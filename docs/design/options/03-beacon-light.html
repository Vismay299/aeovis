How a reading is made

Four moves. No configuration.

Enter a domain and everything below happens on its own. The tracked question set is frozen per domain, so any two readings are always comparable.

01

Your category is inferred from your site

Not from a form you fill in, and not from keywords you nominate. If the inference is wrong, the reading would be wrong — so you can see and edit exactly what we concluded.

02

36 unbranded questions are written

Every one is a phrasing a buyer would actually type, and not one of them contains your name. A leading question returns a flattering answer, which is worth nothing.

03

Every engine is asked every question

Google AI Overview, ChatGPT and Perplexity, in parallel — because they disagree, and averaging them hides the engine you are invisible in.

04

The answers are kept, word for word

Who was named, who was cited, and the sentence that proves it. Rivals come out of the answers themselves, so the list is the one the engines actually believe in.

Objections

Fair questions.

The four that come up on every first call, answered without marketing language.

Why not just ask the engine about my brand?

Because a branded question already contains the answer. Ask "is Brightwave good for hospitals" and the engine will find something kind to say — it is a completion machine and you handed it the premise. Your buyer types "best EHR for a 200-bed hospital", and there the engine has to choose.

How is this different from rank tracking?

A rank tracker tells you where a page sits in a list of blue links. There is no list here. There is one paragraph naming two vendors. We measure whether that paragraph says your name, and we keep the paragraph so you can argue with it.

Does the number actually move?

Yes, and that is the whole point of running it on a cadence. Readings are stored per question, per engine, per day, so when a question flips from a rival to you we can point at the page the engine started citing. The tracked set only ever grows additively, so history stays comparable.

Who decides which brands count as rivals?

The engines do. Every brand co-mentioned or cited alongside you is collected and ranked by how often it takes a recommendation you wanted. You never maintain a competitor list — and on a first reading, the surprises are usually the useful part.
