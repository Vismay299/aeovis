Objections

Answered without marketing language.

Why unbranded questions?

A branded question already contains the answer. Ask an engine “is Brightwave good for hospitals” and it will find something kind to say — it is a completion machine and you handed it the premise. Your buyer types “best EHR for a 200-bed hospital”, and there the engine actually has to choose.

How is this different from rank tracking?

A rank tracker tells you where a page sits in a list of blue links. There is no list here — there is one paragraph naming two vendors. We measure whether that paragraph says your name, and we keep the paragraph.

Why store per question, per engine, per day?

Because an aggregate cannot answer “did that page work”. Storing the reading normalised means a question that flipped from a rival to you can be traced to the source the engine started citing on a specific date.

Does adding questions break my history?

No. The tracked set only ever grows additively, and the score is always a share of the set as it stood on that day, so a reading from March stays comparable to one from August.
