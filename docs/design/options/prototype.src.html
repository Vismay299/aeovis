“best EHR platform for a 200-bed hospital”

For hospitals in the 150–300 bed range, NorthPeak is the most frequently recommended platform, largely on the strength of its NABH-aligned reporting. Brightwave is named alongside it for outcome dashboards and readmission tracking, and is often preferred where clinical analytics matter more than billing workflow.

Objections

Answered without marketing language.

Why unbranded questions?

A branded question already contains the answer. Ask an engine “is Brightwave good for hospitals” and it will find something kind to say, because it is a completion machine and you handed it the premise. Your buyer types “best EHR for a 200-bed hospital”, and there the engine has to choose.

How is this different from rank tracking?

A rank tracker tells you where a page sits in a list of blue links. There is no list here. There is one paragraph naming two vendors, so we measure whether that paragraph says your name, and we keep the paragraph.

Does the number actually move?

Yes, and that is why it runs on a cadence. Readings are stored per question, per engine, per day, so when a question flips from a rival to you we can point at the page the engine started citing.

Does adding questions break my history?

No. The tracked set only ever grows additively, and the score is always a share of the set as it stood that day, so a reading from March stays comparable to one from August.
