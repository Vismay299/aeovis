## Runtime changes

- P0 finalization has one originating-job report identity and one SQL transaction for result links, citations, brand judgments, daily metrics and terminal job state. A projection failure rolls back and a retry reconstructs the same identity.
- Private sanitized response evidence is captured before parsing; write failures are durable states. Unknown Google shapes create an operator incident. Tenant-scoped evidence signing checks ownership and retention; operator history exposes attempts, cost uncertainty and recovery.
- Sourcing freezes question purpose, panel/version/equal weights and full market/language/device/session context. Recommendation eligibility is selected before answers. Prompt-cluster uncertainty accompanies rates. An unreviewed panel cannot gain a qualified headline through aggregation. [Metric formulas and numerical examples](../../contracts/measurement-metrics.md).
- Native/gateway wrappers retain actual returned-model identities or explicit unknowns and distinguish unreported search telemetry. Canary and durable attempt envelopes retain actual capture times and evidence IDs. The live pass exposed a Google method-label omission; method/gateway normalization now reads the adapter's explicit Google SERP method rather than defaulting it to a model API.
- Reviewed customer panels have immutable versions, tenant checks and approval. Worker/scheduler scans freeze the approved version. Schedule-window enqueue, bounded catch-up, overlap/block outcomes and finalization are actual database operations, not the earlier in-memory policy helper.
- Stored report loading, authentication, opaque denial, malformed success, backend/offline failure and retry states stay distinct. Partial/failed collection counts survive reload and JSON download. Scan-form completion notices and portal banners use collection outcome rather than execution status; failed collection cannot announce success. Read recovery sends no paid scan request.
- Research snapshots/skipped inputs and current-draft checks bind exact text and dependencies. Edits invalidate checks and approval. Paid rechecks reserve shared budget and use fenced completion. Both approval routes require the current checked version. Pipelines without a fact-check block deliver an unchecked draft requiring the saved-draft check before approval.
- Customer source/opportunity workflows retain exact supporting passages and review versions; they do not infer retrieval or send outreach. A bounded owned-page technical audit records source/HTTP/directive/canonical/structured-data facts and explicitly unknown indexing/rendering/property controls.
- Both publication routes verify meaningful body coverage and record event, immutable version and item/job transitions transactionally. Publication observations use a verified publication boundary, compatible target cohorts and uncertainty; thin samples have no qualified delta. Remaining legacy content-event reports are still open R19 work. [Current Search Console capability check](./GSC-CAPABILITY-CHECK.md).
